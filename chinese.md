Checked **Sunday, July 26, 2026**. Short answer: **yes, there's a real collection —
and it's concentrated at the North Branch** (your closest), in the *Juvenile World
Language Collection* shelf. Catalog records are romanized (pinyin), so search for
English keywords like **"chinese picture book"** — searching Chinese characters
