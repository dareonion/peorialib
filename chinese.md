# Chinese-language kids' books — Peoria Public Library

Checked **Sunday, July 26, 2026**. Short answer: **yes — 25+ titles**, almost all in
the **North Branch** *Juvenile World Language Collection* (your closest branch), with
a few bilingual audio books and one Mandarin board book elsewhere.

**How to find them:** catalog records are romanized (**pinyin**), so search English
keywords — **"chinese picture book"**, **"chinese board book"**, **"chinese story"**.
Searching Chinese characters (绘本) returns nothing. Titles below are pinyin with a
rough English gloss; the exact book is best confirmed on the record or with a
librarian.
